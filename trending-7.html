Agentforce Voice Learns to Say "Hola" (And Five Other Languages)

Salesforce has quietly expanded Agentforce Voice to support Spanish, Italian, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Canadian French — a big step for any organisation running a multilingual customer service operation.

When Agentforce Voice launched, it handled English. That was fine for domestic US deployments, but it put a hard ceiling on where the technology could realistically go. Salesforce has now removed that ceiling by adding six new languages: Spanish, Italian, German, Portuguese, Japanese, and Canadian French.

What exactly is Agentforce Voice? In short, it's the component that lets Agentforce agents handle phone calls — not just chat or email. An agent powered by Agentforce Voice can answer calls, understand what a customer is saying, pull information from Salesforce records, take action (like logging a case or updating an account), and respond in natural spoken language. The idea is to automate routine service calls without forcing customers to navigate a clunky phone menu.

Adding more languages matters for a specific, practical reason. Enterprise orgs frequently have a single Salesforce instance that serves customers in multiple countries. Up until now, deploying Agentforce Voice in a multilingual context meant English-first with workarounds for everything else. The new language support means you can configure a voice agent to operate natively in, say, German for European customers and Japanese for APAC — same underlying Salesforce org, different language configurations per channel.

There are still things to evaluate. Real-world voice AI in a second language can be less accurate than in a first language, and the quality can vary by accent and dialect. Canadian French, for example, differs noticeably from Parisian French, and Salesforce opting to support it separately is a thoughtful detail worth noticing. Testing in your own customer scenarios before rolling out widely is a sensible step.

Why this matters if you're learning Salesforce: Agentforce Voice sits at the intersection of Service Cloud (case management, omnichannel routing) and the Agentforce platform (AI agents, actions, and topics). Understanding how a voice channel connects to those underlying objects — and how languages and locales work in Salesforce — is exactly the kind of multi-layer thinking that architecture and service cloud certifications test.
