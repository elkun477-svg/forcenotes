Salesforce Built AI Agents That Do AI Research. Here's What They Found

Salesforce's research team gave agents a lab coat, a compute budget and one rule: only keep the changes the evidence supports.

Salesforce AI Research has published SFR-AutoR&D, a set of agents that run the research loop on their own. You give them a goal, a codebase and a compute budget. They come up with ideas, write the code, run controlled experiments against a baseline, and keep a change only if the results back it up. Think of it as a very patient grad student that never needs coffee.

Its first real target was SFR-Embedding, the retrieval model behind search in Data Cloud. An embedding model turns text into lists of numbers so the system can find passages with a similar meaning, not just matching keywords. It's what helps Agentforce find the right record or article before it answers. Starting from the open-source EmbeddingGemma 2 model, the agents produced SFR-Embedding v4 in one research cycle of about 80 hours, work Salesforce says usually takes people several weeks. It scored 6.13 points higher on a standard retrieval measure (nDCG@10 across 10 public MTEB retrieval tasks). A second session of about nine hours made it faster too: 36% more throughput and 17% lower latency, with almost no loss in quality.

The system has three parts. AutoR&D-Engineer looks for code and systems improvements, TrainForge tries out training methods and checks them on data held back for testing, and AutoInfra runs the shared GPU training. Salesforce says training data from product use has to pass ethical and legal review, and the new model still has to meet production requirements before customers get it. No release date has been given.

Why this matters if you're learning Salesforce: You won't be training embedding models in your org, but you will be asked how Agentforce finds the right data. Knowing that retrieval quality depends on embeddings and on clean, well-structured data helps you explain why data quality work still matters in an AI project.
