Practise for your Salesforce certification

Original exam-style questions, timed mock exams and instant explanations, mapped to the official exam sections and weightings. Find your weak sections, fix them, and walk in knowing what to expect.

Honest practice, no shortcuts. Every question here was written by ForceNotes for study. These are not real exam questions, and ForceNotes does not use or share leaked exam content ("dumps"). Using real exam questions breaks Salesforce's certification rules and can cost you your certification. Salesforce does not endorse this site, and doing well here does not guarantee a pass.

How to use it

  • Start with a quick quiz to see where you stand, then look at the section table to find your weakest exam sections.
  • Practise by topic for those sections. Read every explanation, including the ones for questions you got right.
  • Take a mock exam when you are consistently above the passing score. It runs on a timer, shows no answers until the end, and lets you flag questions, like the real exam.
  • Review missed questions a few days later. Questions you get wrong come back first.

Your progress stays on your device

Scores and history are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account. Clearing your browser data, or using another device, starts you from zero.

Where to get the official details

Exam length, passing score and sections change with releases. Always check the official exam guide before you book. Each exam page above links to its own guide on Salesforce Help, and Trailhead has free certification prep trails for each of them. Salesforce renamed Sales Cloud Consultant and Service Cloud Consultant to Agentforce Sales Consultant and Agentforce Service Consultant, so look for the new names. The AI Associate exam was retired in February 2026, which is why it is not listed here. Pair this practice with the interview questions, the study notes and the Apex playground.
